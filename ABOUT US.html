About Us

Welcome to AC LUXE STORE – where function meets fashion. My name is CANEO, AMIE E. – The founder and the CEO of this company. We specialize in delivering top-quality products that make life better, easier, and more stylish. Our dedicated team is driven by innovation, customer care, and continuous improvement.

Since our beginning, we’ve remained committed to our values of integrity, creativity, and excellence. Thank you for being part of our journey!


👜 Our Story
Our journey started back in 2010 with a simple idea: to create bags that are not only stylish but also practical and affordable. At that time, we were just a small stall in the local market, driven by passion and determination. We didn’t have much, but we believed that people deserved good-quality products without spending too much. Little by little, through hard work and genuine care for our customers, we began to earn their trust.

By 2013, we reached an important milestone: we were able to open our very first physical store. It was a big step for us and something we were truly proud of. With more space, we expanded our collection to include different kinds of bags, such as handbags, backpacks, and travel bags. We focused on creating products that people could use every day, making sure they were durable while still looking good. In 2017, we decided to take another big step by bringing our business online. This allowed us to reach more customers beyond our local area and made it easier for people to access our products. It was a new challenge, but it also helped us grow even more. We listened closely to feedback and continued improving both our products and our service. Our mission has always been simple: to provide bags that people can rely on in their everyday lives. We want our products to be useful, comfortable, and something our customers can feel good about using. For us, it’s not just about how a bag looks, but also how it helps make daily life a little easier. As we continue to grow, we stay guided by the values that shaped us from the beginning. We believe in being honest with our customers, staying creative with our designs, and always doing our best in everything we create. We also do our part to support local artists and use materials that are better for the environment. No matter how much we grow, we remain committed to the same passion that started our journey.
